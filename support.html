ProMaster OBD Support

Need help with ProMaster OBD? Email eddy.sampley@gmail.com. Include your phone model, iOS or Android version, your adapter model and your vehicle year and engine.

What you need

  • iPhone: a Bluetooth LE (BLE) ELM327-compatible OBD2 adapter. The Vgate iCar Pro BLE is tested. Classic Bluetooth adapters (such as OBDLink MX+) do not work with iPhone.
  • Android: a Bluetooth Classic, BLE or Wi-Fi ELM327-compatible adapter.
  • A vehicle with an OBD2 port. The app is built for the Ram ProMaster (2014 and later, 3.6L with the 62TE transmission) and also reads generic OBD2 data on other vehicles.

Connecting

  1. Plug the adapter into the OBD2 port under the dash and turn the ignition on.
  2. Open the app, go to Setup and tap Connect. Pick your adapter from the list.
  3. No adapter? Use Setup → Demo mode to try the app with sample data.

Common questions

Does the app change anything on my vehicle? No. ProMaster OBD is read-only diagnostics. It reads live data and trouble codes. The only command it sends that changes state is the standard OBD2 "clear codes" request, and only when you tap Clear and confirm.

Some readings show "—". Not every vehicle reports every reading. Manufacturer-specific readings (such as some transmission data) may not be available on all model years.

The adapter does not show up. Make sure Bluetooth is on, the adapter's light is on, and it is not connected to another app or phone.

Safety

Do not operate the app while driving. Set up the display before you drive.
