ProMaster OBD Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

ProMaster OBD ("the app") is an independent app that reads data from your vehicle through an OBD2 adapter you provide. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

This policy covers both the Android app and the iPhone app. Both are read-only, do not require an account, do not collect personal information, and do not send your data to us.

Summary

The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your data. Your vehicle data, trip reports and maintenance records are stored only on your device.

Information the app uses

  • Vehicle data: readings from your OBD2 adapter, such as speed, RPM, temperatures, voltage, fuel economy, transmission data and trouble codes. This data is shown in the app and saved on your device for gauges, graphs, trip reports and maintenance reminders.
  • Location: on Android, if you allow location access, the app uses your device's location to show your position on the map and to measure trips. Location is used on your device and is not sent to us. On older Android versions, location permission is also required by Android to scan for Bluetooth adapters. The iPhone app does not use your location.
  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi: the app uses Bluetooth, and on Android also Wi-Fi, only to connect to your OBD2 adapter. The iPhone app uses Bluetooth LE only.
  • Notifications: used to show the connection status while the app runs in the background and to warn you about readings such as high transmission temperature.

Third-party services

On Android, the map screen uses Google Maps, provided by Google. When you open the map, Google may receive information such as your device's IP address and the map area being viewed, under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The iPhone app does not include a map. Neither app includes analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Sharing data

The app only shares data when you choose to, for example when you export or share a CSV log using your device's share menu. Where that file goes is up to you.

Data storage and deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, deleting the app from your iPhone, or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Safety

Do not operate the app while driving. Set up the display before you drive.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
